ABOUT

MurdaRP is built on a strong foundation of dedicated staff and a tight-knit community that values fair roleplay, respect, and creativity. Our team works hard to keep the server running smoothly while making sure every player feels heard and supported. Whether you’re brand new to FiveM or a seasoned roleplayer, MurdaRP is a place where friendships are made, stories are built, and the city truly feels like home.

We believe in the "Story First" approach. Whether you're a civilian climbing the ladder of our complex economy or a criminal fighting for turf, the interactions you build define your legacy. Our unique Admin Jail system ensures that rules are respected while keeping the community together, allowing players to reflect and improve without losing their place in the city.

BLOOD IN, BLOOD OUT

Our gangs follow strict Perma-Death protocols. Once you're in, the only way out is through the story.

FAIR PLAY GUARANTEED

Mandatory clipping requirements and active staff ensure that scuff and rule-breaks never ruin your immersion.

18+ MATURE CONTENT

A strictly enforced adult environment allowing for complex, gritty storylines without the typical "troll" interference.

THE MURDA STANDARD

"RESPECT THE ROLEPLAY,
OWN YOUR LEGACY."
