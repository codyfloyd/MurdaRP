CORE PROTOCOL ADMIN JAIL & PERM BANS

FAIR COMBAT, RESPECT & DOCUMENTATION

MurdaRP operates on serious roleplay consequence with active admin enforcement. We prioritize Admin Jail and pocket wipes over outright bans to give players space to adjust without losing their character, reserving irreversible bans for severe bad-faith violations.

All interactions require genuine story buildup. Gunplay is restricted on initial encounters in favor of dialogue and fist fights; random executions, cop baiting, and combat logging will not be tolerated. Reports must be filed within 24 hours and require unedited clip evidence.
