RULES

General & Reporting

01

CLIPPING MANDATORY

Minimum 2-minute clips (5 mins suggested) WITH AUDIO required for all reports. No clip, no ticket.

02

REPORTING WINDOW

You have 24 hours after an incident to report it. Small petty tickets or reporting without clips may result in Admin Jail for the reporter.

03

COMMUNITY SAFETY

Strictly 18+. Zero tolerance for harassment of women. Disrespecting staff in tickets results in immediate closure.

Roleplay Standards

04

METAGAMING & POWERGAMING

No outside info (Twitch/Discord). No scuff/glitch abuse. WEAPON RESTRICTION: Putting any weapon in your 6th slot will result in an immediate pocket wipe.

05

HITMAN RP

Allowed ONLY with staff permission. Hits last 72 hours. No interaction needed once approved. No robbing unless in the contract.

06

TORTURE RP

If kidnapped/tortured, you must answer 1 of 3 questions truthfully. Afterward, you must be released or dumped at the hospital/turf.

Interactions & Combat

07

THE 3-STEP ESCALATION

1st: RP/Melee Only. 2nd: Argument/Hand-to-hand. 3rd: Gunplay allowed. Scenes must not last more than 2 days. Settlements/End-games must be reached if one side wants out.

RAMMING is an Admin Jail offense. Use PITS (back wheel) to stop vehicles.
08

WAR RULES

Buildup required. Terms must be discussed with the Cartel. Max duration 5 days. NO POCKET WIPING during war fights. No fighting at businesses.

09

BODY DUMPING (FLAT BACK)

If flat-backed, you only remember the scene if: Reminded by a witness, or the opposition gloats/posts it on social media.

Crime & Heist Limits

10

HEIST CAPACITY

Fleeca: 4 Crim / 6 PD / 1 Swap
Paleto: 6 Crim / 8 PD / 1 Swap
Casino+: 10 Crim / 12 PD / 2 Swaps
Drugs/Boosting: 6 Crim / 10 PD

Shootouts: Max 12 PD can join. 4-minute wait for PD required after any heist.

11

COOLDOWNS

Hefty Crime: 30 mins after break-off. Petty Crime: 15 mins. Shootouts: 40 mins (No interactions/taunting during this time).

PD, EMS, & DOJ

12

LEGAL CORRUPTION

Allowed within limits. PD cannot sell house locations or critical info. EMS cannot revive without RP. PD vehicles can be looted if left UNLOCKED.

13

UNIVERSITY ZONE

WEAPON-FREE ZONE. Shooting here = Permanent Ban. Fighting allowed only if making RP sense (bathrooms/courtyard).
